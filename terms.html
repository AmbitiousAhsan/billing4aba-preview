Terms of Service

These terms govern the services Billing4ABA provides to ABA clinics. The definitive terms for any specific engagement are set out in the signed services agreement and Business Associate Agreement (BAA) between us — this page is a plain-language summary.

This is not legal advice. Please review the signed agreement and consult counsel for your specific situation.

Services

Billing4ABA provides revenue-cycle management services for ABA clinics, which may include: claims submission and follow-up, denial management, payer credentialing, prior authorizations, posting and reconciliation, and weekly reporting. The exact scope is set out in your services agreement.

Fees

Our standard fee structure is:

  • Onboarding: $250 flat, upfront. Covers system integration, past-claims audit, and workflow map.
  • Full RCM (billing & coding): typically around 5% of collected revenue, with a $500/month minimum for smaller clinics. Larger or more complex practices can request a custom quote based on payer mix, volume, and scope.
  • Credentialing: success fee — billed only after a payer approval lands.

Why quote-based pricing? ABA billing scope changes by clinic size, payer mix, EHR, claim volume, credentialing needs, and AR backlog. The free billing review helps us recommend the right scope instead of forcing every clinic into the same package.

Specific rates, minimums, and any custom-scope work are set out in the signed services agreement, which controls if anything here is in conflict.

No long lock-in

We do not require multi-year commitments. Either party may end the engagement on the notice period stated in the services agreement; we'll cooperate on a clean handoff of access and open work.

Client responsibilities

To do the job, we need a few things from you:

  • Timely access to your EHR, clearinghouse, and payer portals
  • Accurate session documentation from your clinical team
  • Prompt responses on items that need your sign-off
  • Signed BAA before any PHI is exchanged

Outcomes (collection rates, AR days, credentialing timelines) depend in part on these inputs. We do not guarantee specific financial results.
