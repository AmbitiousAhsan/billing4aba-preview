Chapter 01

What I kept seeing

I’ve spent years around ABA billing, and the same story kept repeating. Brilliant BCBAs — the clinicians families wait months to see — were losing their evenings to denials, credentialing dead ends and aging AR they never had time to chase.

None of it was clinical. It was paperwork nobody owned: an authorization that quietly ran out, a modifier a payer changed, an application waiting on one missing form.

Behind every stuck claim was a session that already happened.

that’s the part I couldn’t let go of.

Chapter 02

Why general billers miss ABA

Most owners I met had tried a general billing company at some point. Their clinic was one account among a hundred — pediatrics, mental health, physical therapy — and ABA, with its units, authorizations, modifiers and payer quirks, was the one nobody on the other end really understood.

That isn’t a knock on anyone. ABA is simply different: hours have to be authorized before they’re delivered, every unit has to match the note, and every payer has its own rules about who can render what.
